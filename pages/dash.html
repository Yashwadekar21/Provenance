Why did we choose GCP over AWS?

Google Cloud Platform (GCP) was selected over Amazon Web Services (AWS) for the following reasons:

  • It was ~20% cheaper based on our projected workloads.
  • It integrated better with our Kubernetes deployment pipeline.
  • It was easier for our existing DevOps team to manage.
